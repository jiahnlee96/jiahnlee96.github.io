Research

Working papers and research in progress

International finance

The Currency Forward Rate Bias of Emerging Market Economies and the Original Sin

With JungJae Park, Saerang Song

Summary

This paper shows that higher foreign exchange reserves increase a currency’s forward rate bias (FRB), improving currency safety and encouraging greater issuance of local-currency sovereign debt, thereby mitigating emerging markets’ “original sin.”

Health & industrial organization

Identifying Sources of Inefficiency in Cesarean Delivery: Evidence from Korea

With Yoonji Choi, Yongwoo Park

Summary

This study examines whether hospital-level variation in cesarean delivery in Korea reflects comparative advantage or allocative inefficiency, using nationwide health insurance data and a Roy-model-based framework.

AI & labor markets

The direction of AI innovation and occupational employment: Patent-based evidence from Korea

Sole-authored

Submitted to Labour Economics

Summary

This paper shows that the direction of AI innovation matters for employment: expertise-leveling AI is associated with weaker high-skill employment, while assistance-oriented AI is linked to stronger middle-skill employment, even when overall AI innovation is held constant.

Contribution. Patent-data construction, industry matching, and empirical analysis using an LLM-based approach.

Industrial organization

Spatial Competition in the Plastic Surgery Retail Market: Evidence from a Korean Plastic Surgery Platform

With InKyoung Kim (Sogang University)

Summary

Spatial analysis of competition between plastic-surgery providers (GP versus SP) using evidence from a Korean plastic-surgery online platform.
